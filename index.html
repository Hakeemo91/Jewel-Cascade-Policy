Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

Jewel Cascade ("the Game") is a mobile game developed by Mahmoud Abdelhakeem. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect Directly

The developer does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that directly identifies you. Game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements provided by AppLovin. To serve and measure those ads, AppLovin and its demand partners may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked

This information is collected by AppLovin directly and is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

3. Your Choices

You can limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then disable tracking for the Game

Where required by law, the Game will ask for your consent before personalized advertising is enabled, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

4. Permissions

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or files.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us and it will be deleted.

6. Data Security

Because the developer does not collect or store personal data, there is no developer-held database that could be exposed. Information handled by AppLovin is protected under its own security practices.

7. Data Retention

The developer retains no user data. Retention of advertising data is determined by AppLovin, as described in its privacy policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be reflected on this page and in the app store listing.
