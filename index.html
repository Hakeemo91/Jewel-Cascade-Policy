Jewel Cascade – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Jewel Cascade is a single-player puzzle game developed by Mahmoud Abdelhakeem ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on iOS or Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or any other information that directly identifies you. The game has no accounts and no sign-in.

Your settings, scores, records and any unfinished game are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and deleting the app removes them.

Advertising

The game shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • device information (model, operating system, language)
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. How Google uses it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later.
  • On any device you can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings: on iPhone under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and on Android under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location or files. It uses an internet connection only to load ads.

Children

Jewel Cascade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

We do not store any user data on our own servers, so we hold no database of players. Data collected for advertising is kept by Google under its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
